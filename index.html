I'm a second-year Ph.D. student at the Stanford Physical and Spatial Intelligence Lab, advised by Prof. Gordon Wetzstein.

I study the fundamentals and applications of diffusion models, from fundamentals for efficient generation to long-context generation, causal video generation, and physical AI.

Previously, I worked on single-photon imaging with Prof. Kyros Kutulakos and Prof. David Lindell.
